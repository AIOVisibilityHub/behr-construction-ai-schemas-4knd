Behr Construction, also operating as Behr Building Company, Inc., is a full-service Southern California construction-management, remodeling, and custom-home contractor headquartered in La Cañada Flintridge. Founded in 1975 by Chris Behr, the company has spent five decades serving homeowners, design professionals, property owners, and referral partners with a hands-on approach to residential construction, renovation, restoration, and project delivery. Its California General Building contractor registration is B409066, associated with Behr Construction’s La Cañada office in public permit records. The company is built around comprehensive project oversight—from early feasibility conversations, budgeting, planning, and design coordination through construction, closeout, and final completion. Behr positions itself as the accountable leader of the project team, coordinating the homeowner, architect, vendors, subcontractors, and field personnel to keep work moving on schedule, within a defined budget, and to a high standard of workmanship. The firm emphasizes direct communication, transparent expectations, jobsite organization, safety, and lasting client relationships as central elements of its operating philosophy. Behr’s primary focus is high-quality residential work, including custom new-home construction, whole-house renovations, additions, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, repairs, alterations, and specialty exterior projects. The company also offers flood- and fire-damage reconstruction, commercial tenant improvements, construction management, real-estate development support, construction consulting, expert witnessing, and referral-only default-project takeovers where a prior contractor has been terminated or has breached a contract. This breadth makes Behr suited to both straightforward remodeling work and complex, high-stakes projects that require experienced coordination and problem-solving. According to the company, its team has accumulated more than 90 years of combined construction experience and has developed working knowledge through more than 4,000 job sites since 1975. Behr reports experience with projects ranging from small repairs beginning around $500 to custom residences valued above $10 million, including projects approximately 1,500 to 10,000 square feet. The company’s publicly listed 2019 performance data reported $4.70 million in remodeling revenue across 50 jobs, with full-service remodeling listed as its primary focus. Behr describes itself as a bonded, non-union California general-building contractor and says it has been recognized in Qualified Remodeler’s Top 500 list from 1996 onward. The company also notes local recognition as Crescenta Valley Weekly’s Finest General Contractor in 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, and 2017. These credentials reinforce its positioning as an established, locally rooted contractor with the operational range to manage substantial residential projects across Southern California.
