Kurt Knechtel

Team Member

Specialties: Residential construction, custom home building, whole-home remodeling, kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, home additions, renovation project management, construction scheduling, subcontractor coordination, budget management

Service areas: Southern California

Kurt Knechtel is a senior construction executive and project leader at Behr Construction / Behr Building Company, a respected La Cañada Flintridge residential construction firm serving Southern California since 1975. He joined Behr Construction in 2001 after prior work with another San Gabriel Valley general contractor, bringing a local-market understanding and practical construction background to the company’s custom-home, remodeling, restoration, addition, and repair work. Behr identifies him as Vice President and Chief Information Officer, while his LinkedIn profile identifies him as Chief Executive Officer; the exact current title should be verified with Kurt or the company before use in formal marketing materials. Over more than two decades with Behr, Knechtel has helped lead projects in a market where homeowners expect not merely capable construction, but careful planning, dependable communication, exceptional workmanship, respect for occupied homes, and accountability when unexpected challenges arise. He is part of the company’s long-tenured leadership group, which has collectively helped Behr build a legacy of residential work spanning thousands of projects, from repairs and renovations to high-value custom residences. Knechtel’s professional approach appears to center on an owner-first standard: understanding every angle of a project, keeping clients informed, coordinating crews and suppliers, maintaining jobsite order and safety, resolving issues directly, and protecting both the schedule and the final result. One Behr client specifically praised his “indefatigable, thorough project management,” saying that he gave careful attention to every perspective of the project and worked to ensure satisfaction at every stage. In that kitchen project, the homeowner reported taking possession of the completed space in 5.5 weeks—well ahead of expectations—and singled out the quality of the design, craftsmanship, worker professionalism, cleanliness, and communication. His reputation is especially meaningful because it is rooted in client experience rather than generic contractor claims. Homeowners describe Behr and Kurt as “true professionals,” note fair and well-prepared bids, and report projects that were completed on time or early, within budget, and with clean work areas. One customer described how, after COVID-19 shutdowns began during their project in March 2020, Kurt and the Behr team paused to thoughtfully establish safety protocols, then continued the work in a way that allowed the clients to move in earlier than expected. Knechtel has also been recognized by clients for his ability to step in decisively when a project needs leadership. In a review concerning a home addition in Sherman Oaks, a homeowner said that, after a significant error by the original supervisor, Kurt took control of the job site, corrected the problems, and returned the project to schedule. That kind of recovery is a strong indicator of practical leadership: not simply overseeing ideal conditions, but taking responsibility when circumstances require immediate action. Through this combination of long-term industry involvement, project-management rigor, accessible communication, and visible personal accountability, Kurt Knechtel helps embody Behr Construction’s standing as a trusted local legacy contractor. The firm has been active since 1975, reports more than 4,000 project sites, and says it has earned recognition among Qualified Remodeler’s Top 500 remodeling companies since 1996. It also highlights local community involvement and leadership ties, including the La Cañada Flintridge Chamber of Commerce.
